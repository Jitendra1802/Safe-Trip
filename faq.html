Booking & Reservations

How do I book a trip with Safe Trip? ▼
Booking with Safe Trip is easy! Simply browse our available trips on the homepage, select your preferred destination, click "Book Now", and fill in the registration form with your details. You can also call our customer support team at +91 80123 45678 for assistance.
Can I modify my booking after confirmation? ▼
Yes, you can modify your booking up to 72 hours before the travel date. Changes are subject to availability and may incur additional charges. Contact our support team with your booking reference number for assistance.
Do you offer group booking discounts? ▼
Yes! We offer special discounts for groups of 4 or more people. The discount percentage varies based on group size and destination. Please contact our group booking team at groups@safetrip.com for a customized quote.

Payment & Pricing

What payment methods do you accept? ▼
We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), net banking, UPI payments, PayPal, and digital wallets like PayTM and Google Pay. We also offer EMI options on select credit cards for bookings above ₹10,000.
Are there any hidden charges? ▼
No, we believe in complete transparency. The price you see includes all taxes and fees. However, personal expenses, additional activities not mentioned in the itinerary, and tips are not included.
Is my payment information secure? ▼
Absolutely! We use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect your payment information. We are PCI DSS compliant and never store your card details on our servers. All transactions are processed through secure payment gateways.

Cancellation & Refunds

What is your cancellation policy? ▼
Our cancellation policy varies by package type:
• Standard packages: 15% cancellation fee up to 7 days before travel
• Premium packages: 10% cancellation fee up to 14 days before travel
• International packages: 20% cancellation fee up to 21 days before travel
No refund is available for cancellations made within 48 hours of travel.
How long does it take to process a refund? ▼
Refunds are typically processed within 7-10 working days. The time taken for the amount to reflect in your account depends on your bank or payment provider. Credit card refunds may take 3-5 additional days.
Can I transfer my booking to someone else? ▼
Yes, you can transfer your booking to another person up to 72 hours before the travel date. A nominal transfer fee of ₹500 applies, and the new traveler must meet all eligibility requirements. Contact our support team for assistance with transfers.

Safety & Security

How do you ensure traveler safety? ▼
Safety is our top priority! We conduct thorough background checks on all partners, provide 24/7 emergency support, include travel insurance in all packages, conduct regular safety audits, and provide detailed safety briefings before each trip.
What happens in case of emergency? ▼
In case of any emergency, we have a dedicated 24/7 helpline (+91 90123 45678). Our team provides immediate assistance, coordinates with local authorities, arranges medical support if needed, and keeps your family informed as per your instructions.
Is travel insurance included? ▼
Yes, comprehensive travel insurance is included in all our packages. This covers medical emergencies, trip cancellations, lost baggage, and other unforeseen circumstances. You'll receive the insurance details along with your booking confirmation.

Travel Information

What documents do I need for travel? ▼
Required documents vary by destination:
• Domestic trips: Valid ID proof (Aadhar, Voter ID, Passport)
• International trips: Valid passport with minimum 6 months validity, visa (if required), and travel insurance
• Pilgrimage trips: ID proof and any specific documents mentioned in the itinerary
Can you customize travel packages? ▼
Absolutely! We offer fully customized packages based on your preferences, budget, and interests. Simply contact our travel experts with your requirements, and we'll create a personalized itinerary just for you.
What about accommodation quality? ▼
We partner with certified 3-star to 5-star hotels based on package category. All accommodations are verified for safety, hygiene, and service quality. We personally inspect each property before including it in our packages.
